About Drivewayz USA

Drivewayz USA Editorial Team · Last updated: November 18, 2026

Drivewayz USA is an independent guide to driveways: what they cost, which materials suit your climate, what permits you need, and how to hire someone to do the work. It's run by Matt, who works remotely and covers homeowners across the United States.

What we do

  • We publish plain-language guides for homeowners planning, fixing or replacing a driveway.
  • We connect homeowners with local contractors. When you request an estimate, we pass your details to a local contractor in our network who serves your ZIP code.
  • We are not a contractor. We don't pour concrete, lay asphalt or license anyone, so always check a contractor's license and insurance before you hire.

How these guides are made

Drivewayz USA is a small operation that uses automation on purpose. It's how one person can keep 1,001 guides accurate and up to date. Here's the process:

  1. Research and drafting: a freelance writer drafts most guides with help from AI research and drafting tools.
  2. Sourcing rules: every price, code or safety claim needs a named source. If we can't source it, we cut it.
  3. Review: Matt reviews and approves every guide before it goes live.
  4. Automated checks: before each update ships, scripts check for broken links, missing sources, outdated dates and page errors.
  5. Updates: each guide shows the date it was last updated. We revisit pages when prices, codes or best practices change.

Our sourcing rules

  • Local prices come only from named local sources, such as city-level cost data. We don't publish statewide "averages" we can't trace.
  • National prices name the source and the year.
  • Codes, permits and engineering claims link to official sources: state departments of transportation, the Federal Highway Administration, university extension services and industry standards bodies.
  • Prices are planning ranges, not quotes. Your contractor's written estimate is the number that counts.

How we make money

Guides are free to read. We earn money when we connect homeowners with contractors, and through Amazon Associates links on a few product guides. As an Amazon Associate, Drivewayz USA earns from qualifying purchases. Neither affects what our guides recommend.

By the numbers

  • 50 state guides

Found a mistake?

Driveway costs and codes change. If something on this site is wrong or out of date, email privacy@drivewayzusa.co with the page link, and we'll check it and fix it. To get a free estimate instead, use our estimate form.
